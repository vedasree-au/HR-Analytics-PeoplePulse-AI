## Actionable HR recommendations
- Review workload allocation and sustained overtime.
- Conduct structured stay interviews in groups with elevated rates, applying minimum group-size thresholds.
- Investigate satisfaction drivers through confidential surveys and follow-up action plans.
- Review pay equity using comparable role, level, location and experience cohorts.
- Build a quarterly retention scorecard with headcount, leavers and attrition rate.
- Track intervention outcomes over time using actual dated hire and exit records.

## Interpretation guardrails
- The sample is synthetic and deliberately generated for learning.
- Correlation does not establish causation; role, tenure, manager and other factors may confound patterns.
- Show denominators next to percentages and avoid ranking tiny groups.
- Do not use this dataset for operational employment decisions.
